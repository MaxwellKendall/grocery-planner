$4K/month discretionary spend covers groceries, dining out, gas, dates, and other household expenses. $800/month is the grocery sub-target only.

Hospitality is a meaningful household practice with ~$150/month dedicated to it. Hospitality is about affordability (can they afford to feed guests) and time (is the cook free to be present with guests) — not about impressing people. Domino's/Paris Baguette entries on receipts = ministry hospitality spending.
