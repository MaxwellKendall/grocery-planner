Monday Dinner
Southwest Ground Beef & Sweet Potato Skillet
4 servings Prep 5 min Cook 25 min ~$3.00/serving ~32g protein
Ingredients
1 tbsp olive oil
½ cup diced onion
1 lb lean ground beef
5 tsp chili powder, divided (3 tsp + 2 tsp)
2 tsp ground cumin, divided (1 tsp + 1 tsp)
Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
1 tsp grated garlic
2 cups peeled and diced sweet potatoes
14.5 oz can fire roasted diced tomatoes
4 oz canned diced green chiles
¼ cup water
½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
Cilantro for garnish (optional)
Instructions
1
Heat oil in large skillet over medium-high. Add ground beef and onion, crumble as it cooks. Season with 3 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, salt and pepper. When nearly cooked through, add garlic and cook 1 more minute. Remove to a plate and wipe out skillet.
2
Add diced sweet potato, fire roasted tomatoes, green chiles, water, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, salt and pepper to the skillet. Stir together, cover with lid, reduce heat, and simmer about 20 minutes until sweet potatoes are tender.
3
Add beef mixture back in and stir together. Top with shredded cheddar, cover again for 1 minute until cheese melts. Garnish with cilantro and serve.
Tuesday Dinner
Chicken Carnitas — Juicy & Crispy
6 servings Prep 10 min IP: 25 min · SC: 4–10 hr ~$2.25/serving ~38g protein
Ingredients — Chicken
4 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
6 cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
¾ cup fresh orange juice (~2 oranges)
¼ cup fresh lime juice (~2 limes)
1 tbsp cumin
1 tbsp chili powder
2 tsp oregano (fresh or dried)
2 tsp salt
1 tsp chipotle powder
Toppings (to serve)
Romaine lettuce leaves or tortillas
Sliced avocado, cilantro, jalapeños, lime wedges
Instructions
1
Combine orange juice, lime juice, cumin, chili powder, oregano, salt, and chipotle powder in a bowl. Mix well.
2
Place chicken and smashed garlic in Instant Pot or slow cooker. Pour marinade over and toss to coat.
3
Instant Pot
Seal lid and pressure valve. Cook on high pressure for 25 minutes. When done, quick release pressure manually.

Slow Cooker
Cover and cook on low 8–10 hours or high 4–5 hours.
4
Remove chicken and shred with two forks. Reserve the cooking juices.
5
For crispy edges
Set oven to broil. Spread shredded chicken on a sheet pan and spoon cooking juices over the top. Broil 7–10 minutes, flipping halfway, until edges are browned and crispy. Watch closely.
6
Serve in bowls, lettuce leaves, or tortillas with toppings.
Wednesday Dinner
Pork Tenderloin with Dijon Cream Sauce
4 servings Prep 15 min Cook 30 min ~$3.25/serving ~30g protein
Ingredients
1.5 lbs pork tenderloin
2 tsp salt
1 tsp black pepper
2 tbsp olive oil
2 shallots, finely chopped
3 tbsp Dijon mustard
1 cup heavy cream
1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
Yukon gold potatoes (on hand, to serve)
Instructions
1
Preheat oven to 400°F. Start potatoes if serving alongside (boil or roast separately).
2
Pat pork tenderloin completely dry. Season generously all over with salt and pepper.
3
Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high. Sear pork on all sides until golden brown, about 3–4 minutes per side. Remove pork and set aside.
4
In the same skillet, sauté shallots until softened. Stir in Dijon mustard and cook 1 minute.
5
Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream. Add thyme. Stir to combine into a sauce.
6
Return pork to the skillet and spoon sauce over it. Transfer to oven and bake 20–25 minutes, until internal temperature reaches 145°F.
7
Rest 5 minutes before slicing. Serve with sauce spooned over top and potatoes alongside.
Thursday Dinner
Thai Chicken Meatballs in Peanut Sauce
4 servings Prep 10 min Cook 30 min ~$3.25/serving ~30g protein
Ingredients — Meatballs
1.1 lbs ground chicken
1 tsp grated ginger
1 tsp grated garlic
1 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
½ tsp sambal oelek (or chili paste)
1 egg yolk
2 tbsp cornstarch
1 tbsp chopped cilantro
Ingredients — Peanut Sauce & Pan
2 tbsp vegetable oil + 1 tbsp unsalted butter
5 cloves garlic, grated
2 tbsp grated ginger
1 bok choy, chopped
1 tbsp Thai red curry paste
1 tsp sambal oelek
½ cup chicken broth (plus more if needed)
1 can (14 oz) full-fat unsweetened coconut milk
½ cup peanut butter
1 tsp honey
2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
1 tbsp fish sauce
Juice of ½ lime
Ingredients — Coconut Rice
1 cup unsweetened coconut milk
1 cup chicken broth
1 cup jasmine rice
Toppings: toasted peanuts, fried onions, chives, cilantro, chili pepper
Instructions
1
Do all your prep first: chop cilantro and bok choy, grate garlic and ginger for both meatballs and sauce.
2
Combine ground chicken with meatball ingredients (ginger, garlic, soy sauce, sambal, egg yolk, cornstarch, cilantro). Mix just until combined. Roll into 2-tablespoon sized balls.
3
Heat oil and butter in a nonstick skillet over medium. Brown meatballs all over, about 3 minutes. Remove to a clean plate.
4
In the same skillet, add sauce garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute. Add bok choy, cook 1 more minute. Stir in red curry paste and sambal. Deglaze with ½ cup chicken broth, simmer 2 minutes, scraping up browned bits.
5
Add coconut milk and stir in peanut butter. Add honey, lime juice, soy sauce, and fish sauce. Return meatballs to skillet. Simmer 15 minutes (add broth if needed to thin sauce).
6
Coconut rice (start while meatballs simmer)
Bring 1 cup coconut milk + 1 cup chicken broth to a simmer. Add rice, cover, cook on low 10 minutes stirring occasionally. Remove from heat and rest covered for 10 more minutes. Fluff with fork.
7
Serve meatballs and peanut sauce over rice with toppings.
Friday Dinner
Easy Salsa Verde Chicken (Instant Pot)
8 servings Prep 5 min Cook 35 min ~$3.00/serving ~38g protein
Ingredients
2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts and/or thighs
Kosher salt and black pepper
1¾ cups salsa verde (one 16 oz jar)
4 cloves garlic, chopped
1 jalapeño, stemmed, seeded and chopped
2 tsp ground cumin
2 tsp dried Mexican oregano
1 tsp onion powder
4 scallions, sliced
1 small bunch cilantro, finely chopped
1 tbsp honey (optional)
Serve with: rice or tortillas, Greek yogurt/sour cream, diced avocado, pepitas, lime wedges
Instructions
1
Pound chicken breasts to an even ½-inch thickness (cover with plastic wrap and use a rolling pin or tenderizer). Season with ¼ tsp salt and ½ tsp pepper.
2
Add chicken to Instant Pot with salsa verde, garlic, jalapeño, cumin, oregano, and onion powder. Stir to combine. Close and lock lid, seal pressure valve.
3
Cook on high pressure for 8 minutes. Allow natural pressure release for 10 minutes, then flip valve to quick-release remaining pressure. Carefully unlock lid.
4
Shred chicken with two forks into bite-sized pieces directly in the pot.
5
Stir in scallions, cilantro, and honey. Season to taste with salt.
6
Serve over rice or in tortillas with toppings.
