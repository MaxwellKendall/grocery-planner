Saturday prep for Sunday lunch

  1. Cook a double batch of the salsa verde chicken (3 lb). Shred, keep half for Saturday wraps, refrigerate the rest in its sauce.
  2. Make a big pot of rice and refrigerate it.
  3. Make the homemade salsa and the cucumber-tomato salad (both are better after a night in the fridge).
  4. Sunday: reheat the chicken and rice, mash the avocados with lime and salt, and set everything out with beans, chips, Cabot cheddar and lactose-free sour cream.

Recipes

Salsa verde honey-lime chicken · slow cooker · doubled for Sunday
  1. Slow-cook chicken breasts in salsa verde, honey, lime juice and spices.
  2. Shred and let it soak up the sauce for 10 minutes.
  3. Saturday: wrap in tortillas with lettuce and tomato. Sunday: the base of the bowl bar.

Full recipe at Carlsbad Cravings

Homemade salsa · no-cook · 5 minutes
  1. Pulse tomatoes, onion, jalapeños, garlic and cilantro in a blender or food processor.
  2. Season with cumin, salt and lime juice. Chill overnight.

Full recipe at Kim's Cravings

Cucumber, tomato & red onion salad · no-cook
  1. Slice cucumbers, tomatoes and a little red onion.
  2. Toss with olive oil, red wine vinegar, a dab of Dijon, dill and salt.

Full recipe at Vikalinka

Instant Pot chicken meatballs & marinara · makes ~30 meatballs · Wed + lunches
  1. Mix ground chicken with almond flour, salt, garlic and onion powder and dried herbs; roll into balls.
  2. Pour marinara and a little water into the pot, add the meatballs and pressure-cook about 5 minutes.
  3. Serve over pasta.

Full recipe at Whole Kitchen Sink

Ground turkey & sweet potato skillet · one pan · 30 minutes
  1. Brown ground turkey with diced sweet potato, cumin and chipotle chili powder until the potatoes are tender.
  2. Stir in black beans, then top with Cabot cheddar, tomato, green onion, salsa and lactose-free sour cream.

Full recipe at The Schmidty Wife

Coconut curry chicken · crock pot · serves 6
  1. Slow-cook skinless chicken thighs in coconut milk with curry powder, basil, red onion, garlic, ginger and jalapeño.
  2. Thicken the sauce with a little cornstarch, then stir in cilantro. Serve over rice.

Full recipe at The Food Charlatan

Green enchilada chicken soup · slow cooker · serves 8 · covers Sunday
  1. Slow-cook chicken with diced onion, peppers, green chiles, enchilada sauce, broth and spices.
  2. Shred the chicken, then stir in beans, dairy-free cream cheese and lime juice.
  3. Top with cilantro and crushed tortilla chips.

Skillet chicken with white beans & lemon · one pan · serves 4
  1. Brown seasoned chicken thighs skin-side down until crisp, with lemon slices and shallot caramelizing alongside.
  2. Add white beans and kale to the pan and finish in the oven until the chicken is cooked through.

Chipotle-honey chicken tacos · slow cooker · serves 4

  2. Shred, stir in black beans and lime juice.
  3. Serve in warm tortillas with avocado and quick-pickled red onion.
