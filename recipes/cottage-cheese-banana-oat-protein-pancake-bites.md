## Ingredients

- 1 ripe banana, mashed
- 1/2 cup (120g) cottage cheese
- 1/2 cup (50g) rolled oats
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp (1g) cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp (2g) baking powder
- 1/4 tsp (1.5g) salt
- 1/4 cup (45g) mini chocolate chips (optional)
- Cooking spray or butter (for greasing the muffin tin)

## Instructions

1. Preheat to 350°F (175°C) and grease a mini muffin tin.
2. Blend banana, cottage cheese, oats, eggs, vanilla, cinnamon, baking powder, and salt until smooth.
3. Stir in mini chocolate chips if using.
4. Pour batter into the muffin tin, filling each cup 3/4 full.
5. Bake for 12–15 minutes until golden and puffed.
6. Cool for 5 minutes before removing and serving.
