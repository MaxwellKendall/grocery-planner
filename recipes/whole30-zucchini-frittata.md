## Ingredients

- 1 large zucchini, shredded
- 1/2 pound of breakfast sausage, cooked and crumbled (optional)
- 6 eggs
- 1 Tablespoon of onion powder
- 14 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 tomato, sliced
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350º
2. Combine all ingredients, beating eggs in with a fork
3. Pour into a greased 8” baking dish
4. Bake for 25 minutes or until set in the middle &#8211; it will still be moist from the zucchini, but shouldn’t be gooey from uncooked egg
5. Remove from oven and let cool for 5-10 minutes before serving
6. If desired, top with salsa, avocado, etc.
7. Enjoy!
