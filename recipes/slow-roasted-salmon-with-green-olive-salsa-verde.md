## Ingredients

- 2 lb center-cut filet of salmon (skin on)
- 1/4 c olive oil
- zest of one lemon
- salt and pepper
- 1 c castelvetrano olives
- 1/4 c olive oil
- 1 bunch flat-leaf Italian parsley (roughly chopped )
- 3 whole  scallions (thinly sliced )
- juice from one lemon
- salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 250. Place the salmon in a baking dish, skin side down, and drizzle with olive oil. Season the fish with salt, pepper and lemon zest. Bake for 35-40 minutes until the flesh is opaque and center of the fish is firm to the touch. To check if the fish is cooked, take a metal skewer, or the tip of a knife and stick it into the thickest part of the fish, count to 5 then carefully take the skewer out and hold it against your lip –if it’s nice and warm, the fish is cooked.
2. While the fish is baking prepare the green olive salsa verde. Use the flat edge of a knife to smash the olives one at a time and remove the pits. Place the pitted olives in a bowl and toss them with the olive oil, lemon juice, green onion, and parsley. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Pour the salsa verde over the salmon as soon as it comes out of the oven. The salmon can be served warm or room temperature.
