## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs pork tenderloin
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 shallots, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and prepare a baking dish with nonstick spray.
2. Pat the pork tenderloin dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. In an oven-safe skillet, heat olive oil over medium-high heat. Sear the pork on all sides until golden brown (about 3-4 minutes per side).
4. Remove the pork from the skillet and set aside. In the same pan, sauté shallots until softened, then stir in Dijon mustard for one minute.
5. Add chicken broth and heavy cream; mix well to create the sauce.
6. Return the pork to the skillet, spoon sauce over it, and bake for 20-25 minutes until it reaches an internal temperature of at least 145°F (63°C).
7. Let rest for five minutes before slicing and serving with sauce drizzled on top.
