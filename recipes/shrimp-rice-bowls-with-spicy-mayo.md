## Ingredients

- 7 frozen shrimp 
- 1/2 cup jasmine rice 
- 1/4 cup pickled onions
- 1/4 cup shredded carrots
- 1/4 cup cucumber
- 1 tbsp fresh cilantro
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley 
- 1 tbsp olive oil 
- 1/2 tsp sea salt, pepper
- Spicy Mayo:
- 1/4 cup mayo 
- 1 1/2 tbsp sriracha
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 tsp honey 
- 1/4 tsp sea salt

## Instructions

1. Saute your shrimp by adding olive oil to a pan to heat up. Add shrimp and top with salt and pepper. Cook until shrimp are fully cooked through.
2. Prepare jasmine rice accordingly. *I used a frozen rice to reheat!
3. Make your spicy mayo by combining mayo, sriracha, honey, lemon and sea salt in a small bowl. Mix together to combine. Set aside.
4. In a bowl, add jasmine rice, carrots, cucumber, pickled onions and sauteed shrimp.
5. Top with fresh herbs like cilantro and parsley.
6. Drizzle with spicy mayo sauce.
7. Enjoy!
