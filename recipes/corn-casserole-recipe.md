## Ingredients

- 1 (15-ounce) can whole kernel corn (drained)
- 1 (14.75-ounce) can creamed corn
- 1 (8.5-ounce) package dry cornbread mix
- 1 cup sour cream
- ½ cup salted butter (melted)
- 2  eggs (beaten)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 9x9-inch baking dish and set aside.
2. In a medium bowl, mix whole corn, creamed corn, cornbread mix, sour cream, butter, and eggs until well combined. Pour into the greased baking dish.
3. Bake for 45-50 minutes or until a toothpick inserted in the center is clean.
