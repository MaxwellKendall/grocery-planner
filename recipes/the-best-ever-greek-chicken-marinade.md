## Ingredients

- 1/4 Cup Olive Oil
- 1  Lemon (juiced)
- 2  Garlic Cloves
- 2 Teaspoons Thyme
- 1 Teaspoon Dijon Mustard
- 1 Teaspoon Salt

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk together all of the ingredients for the marinade until they&#39;re fully combined.
2. Then, pour your marinade over the chicken.
3. Store your chicken in the fridge for a minimum of 1-2 hours, and a maximum of 24 hours.
4. Cook chicken according to your preferred method, and enjoy!
5. Store any leftovers in the fridge, for up to one week.
