## Ingredients

- 2 19 oz  cans of lentils, (drained and rinsed)
- 1  cucumber, (halved and sliced)
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes, (halved)
- 1/4  medium red onion, (diced)
- 1 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 2 cups finely chopped fresh parsley
- 2/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- Juice of one lemon
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1  garlic clove, (minced)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Combine all of the ingredients for the salad in a large bowl.
2. Mix the ingredients for the dressing in a small bowl or jar. Pour desired amount over the salad and toss to combine (I only used half the dressing).
3. For optimal taste, allow to sit in fridge for at least half an hour to allow flavours to come together.
