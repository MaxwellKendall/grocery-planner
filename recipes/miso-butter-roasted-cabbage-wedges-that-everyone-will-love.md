## Ingredients

- 1 head Sweetheart (Hispi) Cabbage (Serves as the main vegetable; its sweetness shines through when roasted.)
- 3 tablespoons Butter (Provides richness and assists in browning; olive oil can be used for a vegan alternative.)
- 3 tablespoons Miso Paste (Adds umami flavor and depth; try red or brown miso for a bolder taste.)
- 1 teaspoon Garlic Powder (Enhances flavor with a subtle garlic note; fresh minced garlic can be substituted at the same quantity.)
- 1 cup Panko Breadcrumbs (Adds a delightful crunch to the dish; use regular or gluten-free breadcrumbs if desired.)
- 1/4 cup Chopped Parsley (Adds freshness and a pop of color; substitute with chives if you’d like a different flavor.)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Cut the sweetheart cabbage into quarters, keeping the core intact.
3. Mix together 2 tablespoons of softened butter and the miso paste in a bowl. Brush this mixture generously over all sides of each cabbage wedge.
4. Arrange the cabbage on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast them in your preheated oven for 30-35 minutes.
5. In the last few minutes of roasting, melt the remaining tablespoon of butter in a skillet. Add the panko breadcrumbs and garlic powder, stirring until golden brown.
6. Sprinkle the toasted breadcrumbs over the roasted cabbage wedges before serving. Optionally, garnish with freshly chopped parsley.
