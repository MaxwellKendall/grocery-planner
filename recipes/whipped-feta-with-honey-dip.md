## Ingredients

- 4 oz feta cheese
- 4 oz cream cheese, room temperature
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- ½ teaspoon minced garlic
- ½ teaspoon dried basil
- ¼ cup honey
- 1 tablespoon chopped pistachios
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped chives
- ½ teaspoon cracked black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a food processor, combine feta, cream cheese, and olive oil until well blended and smooth. Add dried basil and minced garlic, mixing thoroughly.
2. Transfer the cheese mixture to a shallow bowl or small plate. Use a large spoon to create a well in the center of the cheese. Pour the honey into the well. Garnish with chopped chives, pistachios, and cracked black pepper around the edges.
