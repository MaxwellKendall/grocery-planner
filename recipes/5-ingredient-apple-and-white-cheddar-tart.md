## Ingredients

- 1  apple (sliced)
- 3 oz white cheddar (shredded)
- 1  sheet puff pastry (thawed)
- Honey (for serving)
- Fresh thyme (for serving)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400ºF.
2. Roll the thawed puff pastry on a lined baking sheet. Spread white cheddar around puff pastry, leaving a 1in border on all sides.
3. Place apples over cheddar. Cook pastry for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the puff pastry is browned.
4. Drizzle with honey and garnish with fresh thyme.
