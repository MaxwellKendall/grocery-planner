## Ingredients

- 3-4 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 2 cups water
- 1 cup honey
- 1-2 tbsp rosemary syrup
- 2 tbsp blood orange juice (any citrus can be substituted)
- champagne (or sparkling water for a mocktail)
- rosemary sprig and blood orange slice (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Combine the water and rosemary sprigs in a pot and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and let simmer for 15-20 minutes, then remove from the heat and let cool to room temperature.
2. Strain out the rosemary sprigs. You should have about one cup of rosemary infusion left. Stir in the honey.
3. Transfer to a jar, cover and store in the refrigerator until ready to use.
4. Put 1-2 tablespoons of the rosemary syrup into each glass (depends on how sweet you want it). Then add the orange juice and stir a bit to combine.
5. Fill the glass with champagne (or sparkling water for a mocktail), tilting the glass as you pour to minimize foamy bubbles.
6. Garnish with a rosemary sprig and an orange slice and enjoy. Cheers!
